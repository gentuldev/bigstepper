Frequently Asked Questions

Do you deliver across Nigeria?
Yes — we deliver nationwide. Delivery time and fees vary by state.
How do I order?
Tap the “Chat on WhatsApp” button and send your size and preferred color.
What sizes do you stock?
We usually stock EU 36–46. You can ask us directly if your size isn’t listed.
What payment options are available?
We accept all kinds of payment — transfers, debit cards, and cash on delivery (selected locations).
Do you offer returns or exchanges?
Yes — exchanges are allowed within 48 hours of delivery if the size doesn’t fit.
